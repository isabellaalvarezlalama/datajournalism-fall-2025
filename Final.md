## Interesting question to answer: How has music consumption revenue distribution changed throughout the years? Considering the introduction of streaming and digital downloads and retention of physical media.

**_Findings from data and proposed nut graf_**

The music industry is constantly facing changes because of new technology, trends, and more. We can see those changes reflected in different aspects specifically in venue distributions. In past years, streaming has brought home most of the revenue from music consumption, however that does not mean that physical media has become obsolete. It is the opposite actually because physical media is still relevant as many people still like to collect it and the revenue from it represents a significant part of the total consumption revenue. 

As a whole, the industry continues to grow its revenue, but the categories within are shifting. Findings from yearly music industry revenue reports from renowned organizations: Recording Industry Association of America and International Federation of the Phonographic Industry show a steady increase of streaming revenue, a plateaued revenue from physical sales, and a decreasing digital downloads revenue. Throughout music history, a certain category has dominated music consumption revenue due to trends and new technologies and software. Today, streaming is still increasing, but that has not eliminated physical revenue as a result, it has just lowered it, but it stays relevant.
